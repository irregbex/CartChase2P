C code of the Cart Chase 2P mode: two-game race (player context, second
cart, second camera, split render, rules, HUD), stages, in-game editor and
recipe compiler. Everything here is behind the --cart2p flag (default off):
without it, DKC1Recomp runs exactly as the original.

Docs: cart2p\docs\CARRERA_2P.txt (race), cart2p\docs\FORMATO_ETAPAS.txt
(file formats), cart2p\docs\EDITOR_ETAPAS.txt (editor),
cart2p\docs\MAPA_WRAM_2P.txt (memory map).
