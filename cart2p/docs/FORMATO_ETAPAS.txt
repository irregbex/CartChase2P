CART CHASE 2P - STAGE FORMAT (RECIPES)
======================================
Created: 2026-10-08. For anyone editing stages by hand or with the editor,
and for whoever maintains the tools.

1. GENERAL IDEA
---------------
Each new stage is built from pieces of one of the two mine-cart levels of
the original game (Mine Cart Carnage or Mine Cart Madness). There are no
invented graphics or enemies: pieces of the level are reordered and
repeated.

  RECIPE  cart2p\etapas_fuente\<id>.json   the SOURCE: this is what you
                                           edit
  STAGE   cart2p\stages\<id>.c2p           compiled from the recipe; this
                                           is what the game loads (do not
                                           edit)
  MENU    cart2p\etapas.txt                which stages appear in the menu
                                           and in what order

Workflow: edit the recipe (in-game editor, HTML editor or by hand) ->
compile -> play.
  - In-game editor: main menu -> "EDITOR" (saves and compiles on its own).
  - HTML editor (fallback): cart2p\JUGAR.bat option 2.
  - By hand: edit the .json and then JUGAR.bat option 4 (compiles all of
    them, adds the missing ones to the menu and rebuilds the catalog).

Units: 1 COLUMN = 32 pixels wide. X values are measured in pixels from the
start of the stage. Y values are the game's own: they grow UPWARD (screen
row = $200 - Y). Values with $ are hexadecimal.

2. STRUCTURE OF A RECIPE
------------------------
{
  "formato": 1,                     format version
  "id": "e023_carnage_hoyos03",     file name (lowercase, digits, _)
  "nombre": "CARNAGE HOYOS 03",     name shown in the menu (A-Z, 0-9,
                                    space; at most 28 characters)
  "nivel": "carnage",               base level: carnage or madness
  "tema": "jungla",                 optional, Carnage only: graphics of
                                    another world behind the track
                                    (section 9)
  "musica": "fabrica",              optional: song of another level
                                    (section 10)
  "familia": "HOYOS",               free text (groups stages in the
                                    editor and the catalog)
  "dificultad": "DIFICIL",          FACIL / MEDIA / DIFICIL (easy /
                                    medium / hard; informative only)
  "descripcion": "...",             what is special about the stage
  "autor", "creada", "modificada", "semilla", "generacion": informative
                                    (author, created, modified, seed,
                                    generation)
  "notas": "...",                   ideas or to-dos for the next edit
  "estadisticas": {...},            statistics: CALCULATED by the
                                    compiler (do not edit)
  "piezas": [ ... ],                THE STAGE: ordered list of pieces
  "objetos_quitar": [ ... ],        level objects that are removed
  "objetos_agregar": [ ... ],       new objects
  "platanos_quitar": [ ... ],       banana groups that are removed
  "historial": [ "2026-10-08 ..." ] history: what was changed and when
}

3. PIECES
---------
The stage is the sequence of pieces, from left to right.

  {"tipo": "tramo", "desde": 170, "hasta": 195}
      Segment ("tramo"): columns 170 to 194 of the original level, WITH
      their objects (enemies, KONG letters, barrels, bananas). "hasta"
      (to) is not included; "desde" is from.
      If the same segment is repeated, its objects appear only in the
      first copy (the game does not allow the same object twice).

  {"tipo": "recta", "columna": 195, "largo": 3}
      Straight track ("recta"): column 195 of the original (a column of
      flat track) repeated 3 times ("largo" = length). No objects. The
      height is that of that column.

  {"tipo": "hoyo", "hoyos_px": [64], "columnas": [437, 437, 445, 446, 447, 448, 437, 437]}
  {"tipo": "doble", ...}   (two pits)
  {"tipo": "columnas", "columnas": [...]}   (any list)
      Single columns of the original in that order ("hoyo" = pit,
      "doble" = double pit, "columnas" = columns). Pits are made with an
      ORIGINAL PIT EDGE (columns 445-448 above: jump take-off, gap and
      landing) between flat columns at the same height. Do not use loose
      empty columns as a pit: that leaves a wall under the landing track
      where the cart crashes or gets stuck without dying.

  "nota" (in each piece): written by the compiler; describes the piece
  (columns, X in the stage, objects, track heights). No need to edit it.

RULES (checked by the compiler and the editor):
  - The FIRST piece is the starting segment: "desde": 0 and "hasta" >= 48
    (that is where the initial state with the Kongs riding is).
  - The LAST piece is the final segment of the level: "hasta" = number of
    columns of the level (Carnage 588, Madness 468); it contains the exit
    door (finish).
  - At each joint the TRACK MUST MATCH: the height at which a piece exits
    (right edge of its last column) must be the height at which the next
    one enters (left edge of its first column), +-2.
  - Maximum length (game memory): Carnage 600 columns, Madness 580.
  - Objects: at most as many as the original level (Carnage 39, Madness
    78, plus the door). If there are too many, the rightmost ones are lost
    (warning).

VISIBLE SEAMS (not an error, but noticeable; added 2026-10-09):
  A joint leaves a VISIBLE SEAM when the column that would follow in the
  original (last column of the piece + 1) is not identical (layer 1, the
  one with the track and the buildings) to the first column of the next
  piece: the scenery changes abruptly and looks like another stage. The
  110 stages of the batch have none: they only jump between TWIN COLUMNS
  (identical columns in two places of the level, with flat track at the
  same height, away from the start and the finish). The editors mark the
  seams in orange and offer the "SIN CORTE" (no seam) pieces;
  estadisticas.cortes_visibles counts them. Straights, pits and loose
  columns almost always leave a seam.
  Madness far background (layer 2): it is half-speed parallax, so the
  compiler takes it as CONTINUOUS (stage column n = original column n;
  past the end it repeats the last 64) and it does not depend on the
  pieces. In Carnage layer 2 follows each piece (in twin jumps it
  matches).

4. OBJECTS
----------
  "objetos_quitar": [{"pieza": 4, "indice": 12}]
      Removes object number 12 of the original level's list ("indice" =
      index), in piece 5 of the stage ("pieza" = piece; pieces are counted
      from 0 in the file; the editor shows them from 1).

  "objetos_agregar": [{"pieza": 7, "dx": 120, "y": 304, "nombre": "NECKY",
                       "puntero": "$BD61"}]
      Adds an object 120 px from the start of piece 8, at height Y.
      "puntero" (pointer) is the object's template in the game (bank
      $B5): it is taken from an identical object of the level (the editor
      lists them). Objects are anchored to their piece: if the piece
      moves, the object goes with it.

  Object types of the cart levels (by template): ZINGER (wasp), NECKY
  (flying vulture), GNAWTY (beaver), CARRO (enemy cart), LETRA KONG (KONG
  letter), RACIMO (banana bunch), BARRIL DK (DK barrel), CANON (cannon
  barrel), COFRE (chest), FICHA (animal token), BARRIL CONTROL
  (checkpoint barrel), GLOBO (balloon, extra life), SENAL (sign),
  NEUMATICO (tire), CUERDA (rope).

  Enemies from OTHER LEVELS (2026-10-12): any template from another
  level's object list works in "objetos_agregar" (the game loads its
  graphics and palette when it appears). Tested on a cart track (they
  appear and walk or fly): KRITTER $9443 $958D $9B83 $9B9F $9C15
  $9C27 $9C4F $9C7F $953B, KLUMP $98B3 $999B, KLAPTRAP $9703 $96F5 $96BF
  $97D1, KRUSHA $B559 $B587 $B5CB $B5E3, ARMY $A467 $A47B, SLIPPA $9CCF,
  MINI NECKY $B863 $B86D, NECKY $BC87 $BCAB $BCBD, NECKY NUECES (nut-
  throwing Necky) $C703 $C73F, ZINGER $9D21 $9D3D $9E1D $9F87 $9FC9,
  GNAWTY $B413 $B3F7.
  Never appear (do not use): Kritter $94ED, Krusha $B5AB and $BB89.
  Ground enemies go at the track's Y height (they follow the slopes);
  flying ones about $30 higher. Do not put added objects at the start
  (Carnage before X $700, Madness before $800): the start already scanned
  that zone when the initial state was saved, and they do not appear.
  The 10 "MUNDO ..." (world) stages (e181-e190,
  tools\cart2p\generar_mundos.py) are examples: theme + music + enemies
  from the same level of the game.

  Invented pits are avoided near flying enemies (Necky, Zinger: from $40
  px before to $200 px after) and ground enemies (Gnawty, carts: +-$80
  px): they attack right during the jump.

5. BANANAS
----------
Banana groups follow their segment (first copy). To remove one:
  "platanos_quitar": [{"pieza": 2, "indice": 5}]
New groups cannot be added (the game builds them from a fixed table).

6. WHAT THE COMPILER DOES (tools\cart2p\etapa_lib.py and, inside the game,
   runner\cart2p\cart2p_receta.c: both produce the same .c2p byte for
   byte; verified with the 110 recipes on 2026-10-09)
-----------------------------------------------------
  - Copies the columns of the two map layers (track and background; the
    Madness background continuous, see VISIBLE SEAMS).
  - Moves objects, bananas and the exit door with their segment.
  - Rebuilds the level's CAMERA ZONES (the camera height per segment) so
    they follow each segment.
  - Writes the .c2p (format v3 below), fills in notes and statistics, and
    adds the stage to the menu if it was not there.
  The game installs the .c2p when the race starts (runner\cart2p\
  cart2p_stage.c): map in WRAM $7F:3800, objects, bananas and camera in
  the in-memory copy of the ROM (restored when the stage changes).

7. BINARY FORMAT .c2p v3 (little endian)
----------------------------------------
  char[8] "C2PSTG3\0"; u16 entrada; u16 columnas; u16 camara_max;
  u16 objetos; u16 grupos_platanos; u16 bytes_metatiles; u16 bytes_objetos;
  u16 bytes_platanos; u16 bytes_zonas_camara; u16 tema y musica (low
  byte: theme, 0 = none, otherwise the position + 1 in the theme list:
  1 jungla ... 8 mina; high byte: music, 0 = the level's own, otherwise
  the game's song + 1, section 10);
  capa1[columnas*32]; capa2[columnas*32]; objetos[bytes_objetos] (8 bytes
  each: type, X, Y, template); platanos[bytes_platanos] (4 bytes each);
  zonas_camara[...] (8 bytes each: X, Y min, Y max, flags; end $FFFF).
  (entrada = entrance, columnas = columns, camara = camera, objetos =
  objects, platanos = bananas, capa = layer, zonas = zones.)
  The game still reads formats 1 and 2 (older stages).

8. TOOLS
--------

  tools\cart2p\compilar_etapas.py compiles recipes, menu and catalog
  tools\cart2p\editor_server.py   server for the HTML editor (cart2p\editor\)
  tools\cart2p\exportar_editor.py draws the levels for the editor
  tools\cart2p\generar_etapas.py  the batch of 110 seamless stages (v2)
  tools\cart2p\gen_stage.py       one automatic stage (seed);
                                  generar_sin_costura and twins
  tools\cart2p\azar_etapas.py     seamless randomizer (JUGAR.bat
                                  option 3; the game has the same in C)
  tools\cart2p\temas.py           graphics of other worlds (section 9)
  Track profiles measured by the game: cart2p\stages\vias_<nivel>.txt
  Catalog of all stages: cart2p\etapas_fuente\CATALOGO_ETAPAS.txt
  (generated by tools\cart2p\compilar_etapas.py)

9. THEMES: GRAPHICS OF OTHER WORLDS (2026-10-09, Madness 2026-10-10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A Carnage or Madness stage can have a "tema" (theme): the same track (and
the same gameplay) with the scenery of another world of the game behind.
  jungla   Jungle Hijinxs        cueva    Bouncy Bonanza
  fabrica  Oil Drum Alley        nieve    Snow Barrel Blast
  arboles  Tree Top Town         bosque   Forest Frenzy
  templo   Temple Tempest        mina     Misty Mine
(Reef and ice are not available: their maps are not 16-cell columns.)
How it works (tools\cart2p\temas.py + runner\cart2p\cart2p_stage.c):
  - temas.py reads each level's graphics from the ROM, without running
    the game: the game's decompressor (B8:96FC) rewritten in Python, the
    graphics lists (F9:A924), palettes (B9:99F1), video mode (F9:A4DC)
    and map set (81:8C66; the metatile bank can be a different one,
    DATA_818BC0). The temple decompresses E7:3BDE before its list; its
    tiles $116-$11B (animated in the game) are left transparent.
  - Layer 1 keeps only the metatiles with collision (rails, posts,
    starting platform): the game's collision does not change. They are
    drawn with a single palette row (the one the track uses most:
    Carnage 7, Madness 0).
  - Layer 2 (which the game draws behind, BG2) is filled with a "window"
    of columns from the theme's level, repeated (chosen so the
    repetition is not noticeable). Its colors are at 70 % (mina at 100 %)
    so the track and the characters stand out. In Madness layer 2
    scrolls at half speed (parallax).
  - Carnage: track and theme share the BG1/BG2 characters ($2000-$57FF,
    896 tiles); its fence background (BG3) is erased. Madness: BG2 has
    its own characters ($6000-$77FF, 384 tiles for the theme) and BG3
    is off. Madness animates palette row 1 every frame (the lights): the
    theme does not use it.
  - The sky is one color per theme.
Files: cart2p\stages\tema_<nombre>.c2t (Carnage) and
tema_<nombre>_madness.c2t (Madness); written by
"python tools\cart2p\temas.py generar"; preview:
"python tools\cart2p\temas.py vista salida.png [madness]". Format .c2t
v2 (little endian):
  char[8] "C2PPIEL2"; u16 entrada; u16 conservar (metatiles with
  collision); u16 window columns; u16 metatiles; u16 track VRAM base;
  u16 track tiles; u16 theme VRAM base; u16 theme tiles (0 = shares the
  track's); u16 marks (bit 0: erase BG3); u16 x3 reserved;
  char[24] title; metatiles[metatiles*32]; window[columns*16 cells u16];
  track and theme tiles [*32] (4bpp); palette[128 u16] (rows 0-7).
The game installs it when loading the stage (after the .c2p) and redraws
the screen. The in-game editor also draws the stage with its theme.
Development tools: DKC1_CART2P_PIEL=archivo.c2t puts a skin ("piel") on
top of DKC1_CART2P_STAGE; DKC1_CART2P_TEMAS=folder dumps the graphics of
other levels from the game (does not work for the compressed ones: that
is why everything is done in Python).

10. MUSIC FROM OTHER LEVELS (2026-10-12)
----------------------------------------
"musica" sets the song of another level. The game changes it when
installing the stage with its own song-change routine (CODE_B99036: stops
the music, uploads that song's instruments to the sound chip after the
common ones, uploads the song and plays it; this is what it does when
entering each level). Sound effects use the common instruments: they do
not change.
  name      song (Define_DKC1_MusicID)      levels that use it
  isla      $00 DK Island Swing             Jungle Hijinxs, Ropey Rampage...
  cueva     $01 Cave Dweller Concert        Bouncy Bonanza, Manic Mincers...
  niebla    $02 Misty Menace                Misty Mine, Stop & Go, Loopy Lights
  agua      $03 Aquatic Ambience            Coral Capers, Clam City...
  carro     $04 Mine Cart Madness           both mine-cart levels
  nieve     $05 Northern Hemispheres        Snow Barrel Blast, Ice Age Alley
  templo    $06 Voices of the Temple        Temple Tempest, Millstone Mayhem
  fabrica   $07 Fear Factory                Oil Drum Alley, Blackout Basement
  minas     $08 Life in the Mines           Platform Perils, Trick Track Trek...
  hielo     $0B Ice Cave Chant              Slipslide Ride
  bosque    $0D Forest Frenzy               Forest Frenzy, Vulture Culture
  bonus     $10 Bonus Room Blitz            bonus rooms
  arboles   $13 Treetop Rock                Tree Top Town, Rope Bridge Rumble
  jefe      $15 Bad Boss Boogie             bosses
  galeon    $18 Gang-Plank Galleon          K. Rool
Same list in etapa_lib.MUSICAS and cart2p_receta.c kMusicas. In the
in-game editor: key M; in the HTML editor: the "Musica" (music) selector.
A theme brings the music of the level its graphics come from (2026-10-12,
requested by the author): jungla isla, cueva cueva, fabrica fabrica,
nieve nieve, arboles arboles, bosque bosque, templo templo, mina niebla
(etapa_lib.TEMA_MUSICA, cart2p_receta.c kTemaMusica). Stages with a theme
(e101-e180 and the "AZAR" (random) stages with a theme) have it in the
recipe; when choosing a theme in the editors or when rolling a random
stage, the music follows it (unless another one was chosen by hand).
